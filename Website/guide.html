How to use Smart Warehouse

Installation, first login and the everyday workflows for warehouse workers and managers.

  1. Install the application
  2. First login
  3. Read the dashboard
  4. Find a product
  5. Confirm a recognition review
  6. Handle purchase recommendations
  7. Manage warehouses and products

1. Install the application

  1. Open the Download page and pick your operating system.
  2. Windows: run the installer and follow the wizard.
  3. macOS: open the disk image and drag Smart Warehouse into Applications.
  4. Linux: make the downloaded file executable and run it.

Installers are not published yet. Until then, developers can run the client from source as described in the repository's Frontend README.

2. First login

  1. Start the application. On first launch, enter the server address provided by your administrator.
  2. Sign in with the username and password your administrator created for you.
  3. What you see depends on your role: warehouse worker or manager / administrator.

3. Read the dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen after login. It shows:

  • Current stock across shelves;
  • Low-stock products that need attention;
  • Recent movements — every stock change with time, shelf, amount and source;
  • Recognition errors waiting for manual review;
  • Purchase recommendations (managers).
Screenshot: dashboard (coming soon)

4. Find a product

  1. Type a SKU or product name into the search field.
  2. Select the product to see every shelf that holds it and the quantity on each.
  3. Open the history tab to see how its stock changed over time.

5. Confirm a recognition review

When camera recognition is not confident enough, stock is not changed automatically. Instead, the result appears in the review queue.

  1. Open the review queue from the dashboard.
  2. Compare the shelf image with the detected product and quantity.
  3. Confirm the result, correct the quantity, or reject it. Only confirmed results update stock.
Screenshot: recognition review (coming soon)

6. Handle purchase recommendations

Managers only. The system forecasts demand from stock history and planned demand, accounting for shelf life, and suggests what to order.

  1. Open a recommendation to see the product, required quantity, required-by date and the explanation.
  2. Confirm, modify the quantity, or reject it.
  3. To plan ahead, add planned demand for a product on a given date — future recommendations will include it.

7. Manage warehouses and products

Administrators can create and edit warehouses, zones, racks and shelves; register shelf devices; and maintain the product catalogue (SKU, name, category, unit, shelf life). When goods arrive, the application recommends a shelf based on capacity and current placement.
