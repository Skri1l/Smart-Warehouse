What it does

Live stock from shelf cameras

Shelf devices upload images, the recognition service counts products, and stock balances update automatically. Low-confidence results go to manual review.

Find any product

Search by SKU or name and see every shelf that holds it, with current quantities and movement history.

Replenishment that respects shelf life

Forecasts combine history and planned demand, and purchase recommendations account for expiry dates. A manager confirms every order.
